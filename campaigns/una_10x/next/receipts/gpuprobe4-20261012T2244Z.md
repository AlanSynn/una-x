## 4. Honesty / boundaries

- **Primitive-level, not fold-level.** The fold remains 0/4 bit-equal vs the
  delivered authority (G11/census): fold outputs are a property of the delivered
  function's compiled reduction lowering, not the arg set alone. Road (a) GO
  makes the exp-cell substitution *primitive-available*; fold-level admission
  still requires its own gated bridge work (and the CPU_ROUTE_RETAINED law,
  no admission domain, no profile change — nothing here feeds the CPU 10x gate).
- Measured on ONE V100 on ONE node/day; other devices/CCs are projections until
  run. Host identification was re-established on the compute node in the same
  job (not assumed from the login node).
- The K<0/SSE2-variant `ki<<45` candidates in libm were decoded only as needed
  to pin the dispatched op order; the bit-level identification (0/41,598) is the
  actual evidence, disassembly is corroboration.
- sallo discipline: first attempt (slurm 13988323) was allocated **cpu-small**
  (salloc without `--gres=gpu:v100:1`; srun step never created, "Requested nodes
  are busy", cancelled after ~14 min) — **no probe ran in it, no device facts
  produced, nothing simulated**; log preserved as
  `gpuprobe4_20261012.cpu-small-aborted.log`. Relaunch with gpuprobe3's exact
  TRES (gpu:v100=1, cpu=4, mem=16G) succeeded.

  idx-boundary bit-walk `_ulp_shift(0.0,-1)` wrapped to NaN — replaced with true

  the gate's uint64 comparison. No gate-domain case was ever miscounted in the
  committed evidence.
