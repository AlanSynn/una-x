## Probe 2 — fold box census + derived exp arguments (`fold_box_census.json`, schema una10x-gpu-fold-box-census-1)

Manifest: cohort_manifest.csv, 32 rows, betas 0.001…0.004, mid 500.0, radius 400.0.
Device libdevice `math.exp` vs the delivered fastmath scalar authority (same proxy as the
committed census probe), max 1 ULP and 0 sign flips everywhere:

| domain | n | n divergent | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| ge box (x = -b·c, 32 betas × 4096-clip grid) | 131,072 | 8,071 | 6.16% |
| gl box (x = -gr·(p-mid), gr grid × 4096-p grid) | 20,480 | 1,109 | 5.42% |
| **rep4000 ACTUAL ge feed** (+β·max(0,d-plateau)) | 178,777 | 10,649 | 5.96% |
| **rep4000 ACTUAL gl feed** (-gr·((d-plateau)-mid)) | 178,777 | 11,087 | 6.20% |
| rep4000 kn-exponential feed (crop, knn=1) | 4,000 | 45 | 1.13% |
| rep4000 kn-logistic feed (crop, knn=1) | 4,000 | 206 | 5.15% |

- The rep4000 exp arguments were derived in float64 from the delivered fold source
  (`Accessibility.py:83-106`, fastmath via `NUMBA_FASTMATH`; G10 bridge mirrors the
  expressions). Divergent args SPREAD across each domain — no clean safe sub-interval:
  ge divergents at |x| 0.0109…1.50 (all 16 histogram bins populated), gl divergents at
  |x| 0.0101…10.0, kn-logistic divergents all at |x| 3.11…4.99 (the nearest-neighbor crop
  region produces the largest positive logistic args), kn-exponential divergents at
  |x| ≤ 0.144.
- **Derivation certification: `derivation_certified: false`** with the named gap:
  "rand96_unrounded exponential kn: bit-verified derived args through the exp-consuming
  statements differ from the delivered fold by 1 ULP (compiled statement lowering, not
  the arg derivation)". Decomposition recorded in the JSON: `arg_bits_identical: true`
  (0 bit differences between the derived args and the delivered fold's own inline
  fastmath construction, all four groups, both small cases — small3_distinct and
  rand96_unrounded), `outputs_bit_equal: false` (the one 1-ULP param-fed kn mismatch).
  The verbatim INLINE re-implementation (statements + args built in the same function)
  reproduces the delivered ge/gl/kn bit-exactly everywhere tested — so the gap is the
  delivered function's compiled reduction lowering, not the argument derivation.
  Certification is a host-only fact (delivered host fold on the compute node's CPU).
- Per-box device-vs-`np.exp` also recorded (ge 10,700; gl 1,549; max 1 ULP, 0 flips).

**Reading (census + certification only, no admission):** the go/no-go input for
Direction-1 road (b) is NO-GO as a standalone mechanism: ~6% of the actual cohort exp
feeds diverge at the primitive level with no runtime-expressible exclusion boundary, and
even zero arg divergence would not deliver fold-output parity because fold bits depend on
the delivered function's compiled sum-tree lowering (the certification gap). What remains
viable: a device exp reconstructed to the host bits on a declared domain, the Direction-2
exp-free split-cell hybrid, or a new reviewed profile accepting non-host bits — the last
being a user-gated scientific decision, never a silent substitution. PLAN_GPU.md §3
updated with the numbers and the road status.
