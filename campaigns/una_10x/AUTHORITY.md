# Goal and delegated authority

## Target
Achieve at least 10x completed, correct jobs per second relative to the strongest current-main
baseline under the SAME declared resource budget, workload, model/profile and output policy.
The 10x gate is an application gate, not a helper, search, or GPU-kernel gate. Margin goal 12x is
an engineering objective, not a forecast. No universal fastest-possible claim is authorized.

Claim scopes are independent: observed-cohort-10x, observed-single-10x, actual-target-10x,
and broader platform qualification. A cohort result does not imply a cold single-job result.
The mandatory primary is frozen at W00. Record every prespecified secondary, not just winners.

## Order of attack
Remove repeated global work -> remove repeated identical routing -> share invariant metrics ->
reduce preparation/serialization traffic -> expose independent scheduling -> native/GPU region
specialization. Existing prototypes motivate S and C; they do not establish API equivalence.

## In scope
S: checked epoch workspaces, terminal-to-destination indexing, exact retained order, conservative
dispatch, trusted fallback. C: profile-specific routing traces, metric dependency identities,
public RunBatch cohort execution, immutable resident data, bounded cache/resume publication.
F: sparse-output flow gradients and parameterized ordered update tapes, not a new flow model.
N/G: native and GPU execution of reduced-work regions, with actual engagement and capability
admission. Completing inherited platform API/science/reliability requirements remains required
for platform-wide completion. See the explicit task-to-program mapping.

## Not authorized
No FP tolerance, silent lower precision, approximate neighbors, graph cropping, radius reduction,
subsetted production destinations, path-count cap reported as complete enumeration, change from
Madina/all-path or K-alternatives to aggregate flow, shortcut-weight reassociation, new relaxation
order, or removal of required outputs, state, validation, warnings, cancellation or durability.
No weak historical sequential comparator against a tuned multiworker candidate. No active
historical evidence rewrites. No claimed raw evidence recoverability from hashes alone.

## Ordinary decisions delegated
Engineers choose private names, buffer layouts, small implementation repairs, portable launch
mechanics, compiler flags satisfying the frozen profile, and limited schedules admitted by the
DAG. No repeated approval requests for ordinary failures. Proof contradictions are not ordinary
failures: isolate the mismatch, retain fallback, reject/narrow the route, and record the cause.
Never widen a fast domain merely because random tests pass.

## Stops and bounded effort
Do not stop at the previous 1.10 improvement gate. Complete both principal investigations and
public integration, then follow the measured residual bottleneck. Per candidate allow two
structurally distinct implementations and one repair/rescreen block; record rejected variants.
The initial campaign heavy-execution envelope is 8 aggregate host-hours (policy, not a duration
promise), shared across builds/tests/benchmarks and all agents. Record consumption. Reserve at
least 25% for final validation. On exhaustion checkpoint target-unmet/blocked honestly; do not
claim success or buy capacity. Progress can resume later only under explicit continuation.

A reviewer can reject a change that fails a proof or actual boundary. A target miss cannot be
fixed by changing the benchmark denominator, reducing the dataset, hiding a slow case, or
lowering the 10x threshold after results. Preserve a useful subtarget change as improvement_only.
